Get Started

Accessing the Codebase

You can work in StackBlitz (browser-based, no install) or clone the repo to your local IDE.

Open Your Team's App

Scan the QR code on your table, or navigate to the StackBlitz link provided. Your team URL includes a ?team= parameter that connects you to your team's shared database.

Explore the App

Browse the plant store — shop for plants, add items to cart, try checking out. Notice anything off? That's a bug. Use browser DevTools (F12) to inspect the console, network tab, and DOM.

Read the Bug Report

Your team will receive a Bug Report with issues reported by stakeholders — some vague, some specific. Match the reports to what you see in the app and the code.

Prioritize & Fix

As a team, decide which bugs to tackle first based on severity and your role's perspective. Make code changes in StackBlitz or your IDE, and verify your fix works.

Prefer your own IDE?

Clone the repository from GitHub and run npm install && npm start. The app is built with Angular and runs locally on localhost:4200.

Triage & Prioritize

Bug Severity Levels

Not every bug is created equal. Use these severity levels to decide which bugs to tackle first as a team.

Critical

Blocker — Feature Completely Broken

No workaround exists. Prevents a primary user flow. Causes severe loss of revenue, data loss, security vulnerabilities, or widespread app crashes.

Example impact: Users can't check out, the cart total is wrong by orders of magnitude, or the app crashes on load.
Major

Behavioral — Badly Frustrating UX

A workaround exists but causes highly frustrating user experience. Frustrated users can still eventually check out, but something important is clearly wrong.

Example impact: The cart doesn't update correctly, a promotional code fails to apply, or a form rejects valid input.
Minor

Cosmetic — Polish & Alignment

Typographical errors or minor alignment issues with negligible impact. The app functions correctly but looks less polished. Users can still shop without friction.

Example impact: A button has the wrong shade of color, a label has a typo, or icons are slightly misaligned.
What You'll Encounter

Bug Categories

The Bug Report contains a variety of issue types. Each requires different debugging tools and approaches.

🎨

Visual / CSS

Layout breaks, z-index conflicts, pop-ups that won't close, mobile responsiveness issues.

🔢

Logic & Calculation

Incorrect totals, wrong price references, off-by-one errors, string math instead of number math.

📋

Form Validation

Overly strict regex, stale state carried between selections, generic error messages that don't help the user.

🗄️

Database / Data

Incorrect data references, sale prices not honored, data fetching errors.

🖱️

Interaction & State

Buttons not responding, double-click registering twice, sold-out items silently added to cart.

📱

Device-Specific

Touch targets too small on mobile, scroll bugs on iOS, viewport-specific rendering issues.

AI as Your Sidekick

AI Debugging Templates

AI agents can accelerate your debugging — but only if you prompt them well. Here are three approaches, from precise to exploratory.

1. Precise Logic Fix

When you've found the exact function or line that's wrong, give the AI full context and ask for a targeted fix.

"Please look at this specific function below and tell me what is causing this bug (e.g., $2010 total). Do not rewrite the entire file, just show the corrected function. [Paste Code]."

2. Narrow Console Error

When you have an error message but aren't sure where it originates, paste the error and ask for likely causes.

"I am getting this error on my console: [Paste Error]. It triggers when I fire this specific method: [Paste Method]. What are the top 3 most likely causes in Angular?"

3. Broad Exploration

When you're stuck and need a starting point, describe the unexpected behavior and let the AI suggest where to look.

"The cart total shows $2,010 instead of $20.10. The relevant files are cart.service.ts and checkout.component.ts. Walk me through the data flow from adding an item to displaying the total, and flag where a string-to-number conversion issue might occur."

Remember: Audit the Output

Stop hallucinations in their tracks. Stress-test the AI's suggestions, push back on assumptions, and force the agent to defend its logic. Did it catch all the edge cases? Own your solution — don't just click "accept."

Your Toolkit

Hints for Success

📝
Document clearly. Write down what you found, how you found it, and your fix. Collaborative notes help the whole team.
🤝
Collaborate often. Share your screen, pair up, and talk through problems. Two perspectives catch more than one.
🃏
Use "Unblocker" cards. Stuck? Use the unblocker cards at your table and ask a facilitator for a nudge in the right direction.
🔍
Use DevTools. The Console, Network tab, and Elements inspector are your best friends. Check for errors, failed requests, and DOM state.
🏆
Own the solution. Don't just fix it — understand why it broke and explain your reasoning to your teammates.
💡
Advocate with intent. Practice justifying technical decisions and driving team priorities based on your role's perspective.
